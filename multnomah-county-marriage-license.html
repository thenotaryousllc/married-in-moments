The Good News Most Couples Miss

Your Multnomah County license works anywhere in Oregon.

An Oregon marriage license isn’t tied to the county that issued it — it’s valid statewide for 60 days. So apply at the Portland office (it’s quick, and no appointment is needed), then say “I do” wherever feels right. For lots of Portland couples, that’s our calm, styled space in Beaverton — no courthouse benches, no case numbers being called, no rushing.

Bonus: at $60, a Multnomah County license is currently $25 cheaper than one from Washington County — and works exactly the same everywhere in Oregon.

Step One: The License

How to Get a Multnomah County Marriage License

  • Where: 501 SE Hawthorne Blvd, Suite 175, Portland, OR 97214 — Monday–Friday, 8:30am–4pm. No appointment needed. Save counter time by applying ahead through the county’s online marriage application.
  • Fee: $60. Cash, credit/debit cards, cashier’s checks, and money orders accepted. Fees can change — confirm on the county’s marriage license page.
  • Bring: both of you, in person, with valid photo ID — plus your birthplaces, parents’ details, and the end date of any prior marriage.
  • The 3-day wait: your license becomes effective three days after you apply. Need it sooner? Ask the county about a waiting-period waiver.
  • Valid for 60 days from the effective date, anywhere in the State of Oregon — including our Beaverton chapel space.
  • Two witnesses are required at the ceremony — bring your own at no cost, or add ours for $25 each.
  • After the ceremony: the signed license goes back to the county within five business days. We handle that for you — priority mailing is included in every package.
  • Questions for the county? Call 503-988-3326 or email clerk@multco.us.

Multnomah County FAQ

Questions Couples Ask Us

  • How much does a marriage license cost in Multnomah County?
    A Multnomah County marriage license costs $60. The office accepts cash, credit and debit cards, cashier’s checks, and money orders. Fees can change, so confirm the current amount on the county’s marriage license page.
  • Where do I get a marriage license in Multnomah County?
    At the Multnomah County marriage license office, 501 SE Hawthorne Blvd, Suite 175, Portland, OR 97214 — open Monday through Friday, 8:30am to 4pm. No appointment is needed, and you can fill out the application online ahead of time to save time at the counter. Both of you must appear in person with valid photo ID.
  • Can we use a Multnomah County license outside of Portland?
    Yes. An Oregon marriage license is valid in every Oregon county for 60 days from its effective date. Many of our couples apply in Portland and get married with us in Beaverton — completely legal, and often easier.
  • Is there a waiting period for a Multnomah County marriage license?
    Yes — like every Oregon county, licenses become effective three days after you apply. If your ceremony can’t wait, ask the county about waiving the waiting period for a small fee. We’re happy to help you time your booking around it.
  • Do we need witnesses to get married in Multnomah County?
    Oregon law requires two witnesses at every marriage ceremony. Bring your own at no cost, or we can provide witnesses for $25 per person — even if it’s just the two of you.
  • Who returns the signed license after the ceremony?
    We do. Your officiant must return the signed license to the issuing county within five business days — we handle that for you with priority mailing, included in every package.
