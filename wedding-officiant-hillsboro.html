Hillsboro FAQ

Questions Couples Ask Us

  • Where do Hillsboro couples get a marriage license?
    Lucky you — the Washington County Recording Office is right in downtown Hillsboro at 155 N First Avenue, Room 130. Start the application online, then both of you visit together to finish. The fee is $85 as of July 1, 2026; because Oregon licenses are valid statewide, you can also apply in Multnomah or Clackamas County for $60 if you prefer.
  • How fast can Hillsboro couples get married?
    Apply for your license in downtown Hillsboro, wait out the three-day period (or pay $5 to have Washington County waive it), then book a time with us — our ceremony space is about 15 minutes away in Beaverton. Many couples go from application to married inside a week.
  • Can you come to us in Hillsboro instead?
    Yes — our Simply Eloped package ($250 flat) brings the ceremony to your home or private location in Hillsboro, with up to 20 guests welcome.
  • Do we need witnesses?
    Oregon law requires two witnesses at every marriage ceremony. Bring your own at no cost, or we can provide witnesses for $25 per person — even if it’s just the two of you.
  • Who returns the signed license after the ceremony?
    We do. Your officiant must return the signed license to the issuing county within five business days — we handle that for you with priority mailing, included in every package.
