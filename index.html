Before Your Big Moment

A Few Oregon Good-to-Knows

  • Bring your Oregon marriage license. You’ll apply through any Oregon county clerk before your appointment — we officiate and sign, but the license is yours to obtain.
  • Plan for Oregon’s 3-day waiting period. Oregon licenses become valid three days after you apply (a judge can waive it for a fee). We’re happy to help you time it right.
  • Two witnesses are required by law. Bringing your own is welcome at no cost — or if it’s just the two of you, we can provide witnesses for $25 per person.
