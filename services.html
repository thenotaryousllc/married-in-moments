Before You Book

What Every Oregon Couple Should Know

  • Your Oregon marriage license. Apply through any Oregon county clerk before your appointment. We officiate and complete the signing — obtaining the license is the couple’s responsibility.
  • The 3-day waiting period. Oregon licenses become valid three days after you apply. A circuit- or county-court judge can waive it for a fee in exigent circumstances.
  • Two witnesses. Oregon law requires two witnesses at every marriage. Bring your own at no cost, or add ours for $25 per person.
  • Add-ons. Customizations and add-ons are welcome — just approve them in advance so your moment is exactly what you pictured.
