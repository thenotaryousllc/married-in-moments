Tigard FAQ

Questions Couples Ask Us

  • How far is Married In Moments from Tigard?
    About 10 minutes. Our styled ceremony space is in neighboring Beaverton, just up Highway 217 — close enough for a lunch-break wedding.
  • Where do Tigard couples get a marriage license?
    Tigard is in Washington County, so the closest office is the Washington County Recording Office in Hillsboro — the fee is $85 as of July 1, 2026. Because an Oregon license is valid statewide, you can also apply in Multnomah or Clackamas County for $60 and still marry with us.
  • Can you come to us in Tigard instead?
    Yes — our Simply Eloped package ($250 flat) brings the ceremony to your home or private location in Tigard, with up to 20 guests welcome.
  • Do we need witnesses?
    Oregon law requires two witnesses at every marriage ceremony. Bring your own at no cost, or we can provide witnesses for $25 per person — even if it’s just the two of you.
  • Who returns the signed license after the ceremony?
    We do. Your officiant must return the signed license to the issuing county within five business days — we handle that for you with priority mailing, included in every package.
