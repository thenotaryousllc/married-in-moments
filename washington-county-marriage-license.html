The Good News Most Couples Miss

An Oregon marriage license works in every Oregon county.

Apply in whichever county is easiest for you — Washington, Multnomah, Clackamas, any of Oregon’s 36 — and your license is valid statewide for 60 days. Live in Hillsboro but your license came from Portland? Perfectly legal. We’re based in Beaverton, so for most Washington County couples, we’re the closest possible “I do.”

One thing worth knowing: as of July 1, 2026, Washington County charges $85 for a license, while neighboring Multnomah and Clackamas counties still charge $60. Since your license works statewide, you’re free to apply wherever it’s easiest — or cheapest.

Step One: The License

How to Get a Washington County Marriage License

  • Where: Washington County Recording Office, 155 N First Avenue, Room 130, Hillsboro, OR 97124. Start your application online at the county’s online marriage application system, then both of you visit the office together to pay and finish.
  • Fee: $85 as of July 1, 2026 (previously $60). Cash, check, money order, and debit/credit accepted (cards add a small processing fee). Fees can change — confirm on the county clerk’s page.
  • The 3-day wait: your license becomes effective three days after you apply. Ceremony sooner than that? The county will waive the wait for an extra $5.
  • Valid for 60 days from the effective date, anywhere in the State of Oregon.
  • You’ll need: valid photo ID, your birthplaces and parents’ details, and the date any prior marriage ended. Both of you must be 18 or older.
  • Two witnesses are required at the ceremony — bring your own at no cost, or add ours for $25 each.
  • After the ceremony: the signed license must be returned to the county within five business days. We handle that for you — priority mailing is included in every package.

Washington County FAQ

Questions Couples Ask Us

  • How much does a marriage license cost in Washington County, Oregon?
    As of July 1, 2026, a Washington County marriage license costs $85 (up from $60). Neighboring Multnomah and Clackamas counties still charge $60 — and because an Oregon license is valid statewide, you can apply in any county and still get married with us in Beaverton. Fees can change, so confirm the current amount on the county clerk’s website.
  • Where do I get a marriage license in Washington County?
    At the Washington County Recording Office, 155 N First Avenue, Room 130, Hillsboro, OR 97124. Start the application online through the county’s marriage application system, then both of you visit the office together to pay the fee and finish the process.
  • Do we have to wait three days after getting our license?
    Yes — Oregon licenses become effective three days after you apply. Washington County will waive the waiting period for an extra $5 if your ceremony is scheduled within those three days. We’re happy to help you time your booking around it.
  • How long is our Oregon marriage license valid?
    60 days from its effective date, and it’s valid in every Oregon county — not just the one that issued it. Apply anywhere in Oregon and marry anywhere in Oregon.
  • Do we need witnesses to get married in Washington County?
    Oregon law requires two witnesses at every marriage ceremony. Bring your own at no cost, or we can provide witnesses for $25 per person — even if it’s just the two of you.
  • Who returns the signed license after the ceremony?
    We do. Your officiant must return the signed license to the issuing county within five business days — we handle that for you with priority mailing, included in every package.
