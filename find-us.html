Finding Us

We’re on the second floor of the big blue “Real Estate” building at 20001 SW Tualatin Valley Hwy, Beaverton, OR 97003. To get in, use the doors closest to TV Highway.

Before you arrive

  • Please arrive about 10 minutes early with your marriage license and your two witnesses.
  • Booked the Drive-Thru “I Do”? Stay in your car in our parking lot and text us when you pull in.
  • If the doors are locked when you arrive, call or text (971) 715-2212 and we’ll come down to let you in.
