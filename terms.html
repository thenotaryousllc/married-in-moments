Terms & Conditions

Please review our policies before booking your ceremony.

By booking services with Married In Moments, you agree to the following terms and conditions.

All services are provided by appointment only. A booking is not confirmed until the required deposit or payment has been received.

Clients are responsible for obtaining a valid Oregon marriage license prior to their appointment. Oregon licenses are subject to a mandatory three-day waiting period after application (which may be waived by a circuit- or county-court judge in exigent circumstances). Married In Moments is not responsible for delays or inability to perform services due to incomplete, invalid, expired, or missing documentation.

Oregon law requires at least two witnesses to be present at the solemnization of a marriage. Clients may bring their own witnesses at no cost. If witnesses are needed, Married In Moments can provide them for a fee of $25 per person, arranged in advance.

Ceremonies and license signings are conducted using pre-written scripts unless otherwise stated. Customizations and add-ons must be approved in advance.

Married In Moments reserves the right to refuse service if a client arrives without proper identification, required documentation, or fails to comply with Oregon marriage laws.

Any changes to services, guest counts, or add-ons must be communicated prior to the appointment and may result in additional fees.

Cancellations, Reschedules & Refund Policy

  • Deposits secure your date and time and apply toward the total cost of your service.
  • No refunds will be issued once services have been rendered.
  • Cancellations or reschedule requests made at least 48 hours before the scheduled appointment qualify for a full deposit refund, or you may transfer your deposit to a new date. Cancellations or reschedule requests made within 48 hours of the appointment will result in forfeiture of the deposit, and rebooking will require a new deposit.
  • Failure to appear for a scheduled appointment without prior notice will be considered a no-show and will result in loss of all payments made.

Late Arrival Policy

Appointments are scheduled carefully to respect every client’s time. If a client arrives late, a $25 late fee will be charged for every 30 minutes past the scheduled appointment time. Services may be shortened or canceled if the delay impacts appointments following yours. If services cannot be completed due to excessive lateness, no refund will be issued.

Clients are responsible for providing accurate and complete information on their marriage license and related documentation. Married In Moments is not liable for errors resulting from incorrect or incomplete information provided by the client.

Marriage is a legal agreement. By booking services, clients acknowledge and accept responsibility for the legal implications of marriage and the accuracy of the information submitted.
