Gresham FAQ

Questions Couples Ask Us

  • Do you serve Gresham couples?
    Yes — and for east-metro couples, our Simply Eloped package is the favorite: we bring the ceremony to your Gresham home or private location for a $250 flat rate, up to 20 guests. Our styled Beaverton chapel space is open to you as well.
  • Where do Gresham couples get a marriage license?
    Gresham is in Multnomah County, so the closest office is at 501 SE Hawthorne Blvd, Suite 175, in Portland — $60, Monday–Friday 8:30am–4pm, no appointment needed. Start the application online to save time at the counter. See our Multnomah County guide for the full walkthrough.
  • Is a Multnomah County license valid outside Gresham?
    Yes. An Oregon marriage license is valid in every Oregon county for 60 days from its effective date — so it works at your Gresham backyard ceremony, our Beaverton space, or anywhere else in Oregon.
  • Do we need witnesses?
    Oregon law requires two witnesses at every marriage ceremony. Bring your own at no cost, or we can provide witnesses for $25 per person — even if it’s just the two of you.
  • Who returns the signed license after the ceremony?
    We do. Your officiant must return the signed license to the issuing county within five business days — we handle that for you with priority mailing, included in every package.
