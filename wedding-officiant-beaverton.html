Beaverton FAQ

Questions Couples Ask Us

  • Where in Beaverton is Married In Moments?
    Our styled ceremony space is at 20001 SW Tualatin Valley Hwy, Beaverton, OR 97003 — everything is by appointment, so book online before you visit. For Simply Eloped, we come to your home or private location instead.
  • Where do Beaverton couples get their marriage license?
    The closest office is the Washington County Recording Office in Hillsboro, about 15 minutes away — the fee there is $85 as of July 1, 2026. Because an Oregon license is valid statewide, you can also apply in Multnomah or Clackamas County, where the fee is currently $60, and still marry with us in Beaverton.
  • How fast can we get married in Beaverton?
    Once your license is effective — three days after you apply, unless the county waives the wait — you can book online and be married the same week. The signing itself takes about five minutes.
  • Do we need witnesses?
    Oregon law requires two witnesses at every marriage ceremony. Bring your own at no cost, or we can provide witnesses for $25 per person — even if it’s just the two of you.
  • Do you take same-day or short-notice bookings?
    We’re by appointment, but our online calendar shows real-time availability — and short-notice openings do happen. If your timing is tight, text or call us at (971) 715-2212 and we’ll do our best to make your moment work.
